# AI Industry News Digest — 21 April 2026

## Companies

## Models

**Gemini Robotics** dropped—a robotic-specific variant that turns multi-step natural-language instructions into zero-shot task plans, removing per-task fine-tuning for factory bots.  
A **7 B-parameter “small” LLM** reached GPT-4 averages on standard benchmarks, proving size can still shrink 10× while staying competitive, foreshadowing cheaper, faster deployments.

## Policy

Illinois lawmakers are fielding a **“targeted AI rule”** push from industry, aiming to pre-empt stricter city-level bias laws and keep Chicago’s AI jobs in-state.

## Open Source

Hugging Face repos for local inference tools (llama.cpp forks) spiked after the 7 B GPT-4-grade leak, signaling another wave of hobbyist fine-tuning and edge deployment.
