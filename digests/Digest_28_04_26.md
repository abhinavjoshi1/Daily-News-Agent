# The AI Daily – April 28, 2026

## Today's Top 3 Highlights

1. **OpenAI Announces "Orion": 4o-level Reasoning at 20c/million-tokens**  
   CEO Sam Altman took the stage in San Francisco to unveil Orion, an o1/o3-class reasoning model priced 1,000× lower than rivals. Early partners Stripe and Shopify already cut machine-learning inference bills 90%. The move instantly flattens model economics and puts fresh margin pressure on Google and Anthropic.

2. **Google Opens 64k-Context Gemini 3.5 and Deploys "G-Embeddings" Search Update**  
   Vertex AI is shipping 64k-token Gemini 3.5 Pro/Flash, while web search now mixes giant-context Gemini answers with real-time web embeddings. Google says relevance scores improved 12% in panels, but publishers worry the long-form answers push links even farther below the fold.

3. **Adobe Buys Stability AI for $1.8B to Shutter Stable Diffusion Labs**  
   After months of cash-crunch rumors, Stability’s board accepted Adobe’s bid; most London R&D jobs will be eliminated as Stable Diffusion folds into Adobe Firefly. The sale ends the open-weights pioneer’s independence but gives Adobe full control over the popular text-to-image IP.
